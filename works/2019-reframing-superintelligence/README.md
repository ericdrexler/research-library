# *Reframing Superintelligence* (2019)

K. Eric Drexler, *Reframing Superintelligence: Comprehensive AI Services as General Intelligence*, Future of Humanity Institute Technical Report 2019-1, University of Oxford, January 2019. Sections were written and circulated as drafts from December 2016. The report argues that general, superintelligent-level capability can take the form of a growing set of AI services, including the service of developing new services, and that this reframes both the prospects for superintelligence and the problems of AI safety.
